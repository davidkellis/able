# Performance Competitiveness: Historical Record

## Status

This is a compact historical index for the former long-form competitiveness
vision. The active goals, scorecard, and candidate gate are in
[performance-competitiveness-vision.md](./performance-competitiveness-vision.md).

## Retained lessons

- Earlier compiler and VM work produced real gains through shared native
  carriers, guarded slot/register behavior, canonical kernel boundaries, and
  reusable host APIs. A gain was retained only after semantic proof and broad
  controls; many local probes were reverted.
- Matrix, Mandelbrot, recursion, Array/text, map/counting, iterator, and async
  measurements are evidence about their dated implementations. They do not
  imply that a related source shape or named type is a current candidate.
- The typechecker-proven primitive-frame experiment, broad raw-cell changes,
  fixed compiled execution-context ABI, and numerous float/cache micro-cuts
  failed broad guards. Their failure is a constraint on future proposals.
- The external canonical stdlib has gained reusable behavior where language
  coverage required it. It must not become a route for benchmark-only APIs.

## Where detail lives

The dated scorecards and profile records in `docs/perf-baselines/`, the VM
historical records, and `PLAN.md`/`v12/LOG.md` preserve commands, samples,
measurements, acceptance decisions, and reversion rationale. Use them to
interpret a source change, never to bypass the active three-application gate.
